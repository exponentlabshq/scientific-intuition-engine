`Fourier transform trigonometric identities same mathematical foundation`

## What was found

Fourier analysis is constitutively built on trigonometric functions, not merely analogous to them: *"Fourier analysis is the study of how general functions may be represented by sums of simpler trigonometric functions, and it grew from the study of Fourier series."* Standard math curriculum (LibreTexts' "Fourier Trigonometric Series" chapters) teaches the Fourier transform explicitly as an extension/application of trigonometric identities, not as an independently-arising structure that happens to parallel them.

## Reasoning

This is a whole-part / foundational-application relationship (trigonometric functions are literally the basis functions of Fourier analysis), not two "habitually incompatible frames of reference" per Koestler's bar.
