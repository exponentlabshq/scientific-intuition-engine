2. **Stable and Unstable Stratification**: Stable stratification occurs when each layer is less dense than the one below it, with buoyancy forces tending to preserve this arrangement. Unstable stratification happens when each layer is denser than the one below it, leading to convection as buoyancy forces cause mixing. ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_and_unstable_stratification?utm_source=openai))
