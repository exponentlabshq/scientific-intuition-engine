## What was found
Zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs) are cryptographic protocols that allow one party (the prover) to convince another party (the verifier) that a statement is true without revealing any information beyond the fact that the statement is true. ([ethereum.org](https://ethereum.org/zero-knowledge-proofs?utm_source=openai)) In distributed ledger technologies, such as Corda, state evolution is managed through immutable states and smart contracts, where each transaction creates a new state that supersedes the previous one, ensuring a transparent and auditable history of state changes. ([educative.io](https://www.educative.io/courses/distributed-systems-practitioners/cordas-data-model?utm_source=openai)) While both ZKPs and distributed ledgers involve protocols to ensure integrity and prevent unauthorized modifications, they serve different purposes: ZKPs are primarily used to prove the validity of a statement without revealing the statement itself, whereas distributed ledgers focus on recording and managing state changes in a decentralized manner.
