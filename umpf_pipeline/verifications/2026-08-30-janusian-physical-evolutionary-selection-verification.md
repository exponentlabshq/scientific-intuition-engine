## Reasoning
The concept of punctuated equilibrium, introduced by Stephen Jay Gould and Niles Eldredge in 1972, posits that species undergo rapid evolutionary changes during speciation events, followed by long periods of stasis. This theory suggests that both gradual and rapid evolutionary changes can occur simultaneously within a species, depending on the context.
