Standing waves are stationary patterns formed by the superposition of two counterpropagating waves of equal frequency and amplitude, resulting in fixed nodes (points of minimum displacement) and antinodes (points of maximum displacement). ([mysimulator.uk](https://www.mysimulator.uk/content/articles/standing-waves-resonance.html?utm_source=openai)) In acoustics, resonance occurs when a system oscillates at maximum amplitude due to the constructive interference of incident and reflected waves, leading to standing waves at specific frequencies. ([cmtext.indiana.edu](https://cmtext.indiana.edu/acoustics/chapter1_resonance.php?utm_source=openai)) These standing waves can both amplify and suppress certain frequencies simultaneously, depending on the position within the medium.
