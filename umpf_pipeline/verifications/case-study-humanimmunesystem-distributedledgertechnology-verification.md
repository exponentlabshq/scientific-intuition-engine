None of it treats the immune system's own decentralized-recognition architecture as structurally analogous to DLT consensus — the search result says so directly: results "focus primarily on practical healthcare applications of blockchain rather than conceptual analogies between immune system function and blockchain architecture."

## Reasoning

A clean case for the rubric's core distinction: "blockchain used in a healthcare context" is not evidence for "the immune system and blockchain share structure" — the two claims are unrelated, and conflating them would be exactly the kind of stretched positive the rubric exists to prevent. Genuinely ambiguous territory; not treated as a pass.
