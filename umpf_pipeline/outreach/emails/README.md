## Phase 4 — independent-convergence outreach (a distinct track, not a Phase 3 substitute)

Different population, different pitch. Phase 3 asks a domain expert directly "is this novel?" for a hypothesis with no known match. This track goes the other direction: `active_research_check.py` (Section 11/12 of the whitepaper) already found a real, specific researcher independently working on the same mechanism a hypothesis proposed — the pitch here is "we landed on your territory independently, no knowledge of your paper," not a novelty question. Sourced 2026-09-01, per direct operator instruction: rank by recency (a 2026-dated match means the researcher is provably still active and reachable, unlike a 1983 paper's author) and by tier (SURVIVES/Contested preferred).

**Real finding while building this list, worth disclosing rather than smoothing over:** the SURVIVES/Contested tiers (the pipeline's highest-confidence output) turned out to have almost no clean 2026-dated candidates. Of 10 total SURVIVES+Contested entries, only one had a 2026 match at all, and its author list ("Yuan Liu, Zhiqiang Zhang, Zhiqiang Liu, Zhiqiang Zhang") contains a literal repeated name — likely an AI-search artifact, not a real 4-author byline — so it was excluded rather than used. A separate top candidate (William Labov, "The Regularity of Regular Sound Change," tagged 2026) was also excluded: the paper's own Cambridge Core content ID embeds `...20005342a`, consistent with a 2020 publication, not 2026 — the AI check's year tag for that match is very likely wrong, caught by inspection rather than trusted at face value. Every draft below survived that same scrutiny; all five come from the Verified/Unrefuted tier (the SURVIVES/Contested pool was too thin and too flawed to fill a clean batch from) with single-or-few, non-duplicated author lists and real, checkable venues (PubMed, SSRN, MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference, arXiv, Springer).

**None of these are send-ready.** `active_research_check.py`'s schema deliberately never invents an email address (same discipline as never inventing a URL, name, or year) — real contact info has to be researched by hand per recipient, the same way Aronson's, Frey's, and Phillips's were, before any of this goes out.
