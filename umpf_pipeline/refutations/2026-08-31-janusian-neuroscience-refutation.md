These are not the same referent; one is a normative developmental process, and the other is an aberration. Thus, the claim equivocates on the term, failing the coherence test.

The prediction remains vague and does not specify how to measure or identify these cognitive deficits, making it difficult to conduct a definitive experiment.

Therefore, the claim does not hold up under the triviality lens.
