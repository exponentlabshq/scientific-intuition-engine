# Adversarial Refutation: Neuroscience (Cortical Reorganization) × Climatology (Thermohaline Circulation)

**Original**: `hypotheses/2026-08-21-neuroscience-cortical-map-reorganization-x-climatology-ocean-current-circulation-thermohaline.md`
**Claim under test** (§4): *"If the functor holds, then significant changes in ocean current patterns due to climate change should exhibit adaptive characteristics similar to cortical reorganization observed in the brain after injury — or vice versa."*

---

## Lens 1 — Coherence: **REFUTED**

The §3 functor equates "evolving neural connections" with "evolving ocean current states" at the Domain layer. This equivocates on *adaptation*: cortical reorganization is teleonomic — activity-dependent synaptic plasticity operating to preserve function after damage. Thermohaline circulation changing under climate forcing is a purely thermodynamic response to altered temperature/salinity gradients — no function is being preserved, no analog to "recovery" exists. The mapping treats "changes over time" as equivalent to "adapts," which are not the same claim.

## Lens 2 — Testability: **REFUTED**

"Adaptive characteristics similar to cortical reorganization" is never operationalized. No metric is proposed (rerouting efficiency, recovery time constant, redundant-pathway formation) that could be measured identically in both a brain and an ocean current and compared quantitatively. As stated, any observed change in circulation patterns could be post-hoc labeled "adaptive" or not — the claim cannot return a clean no.

## Lens 3 — Triviality: **REFUTED**

Stripped of domain vocabulary: "complex system A responds to a perturbation; complex system B also responds to a perturbation; therefore A's response pattern predicts something about B's." This is the umbrella-trap failure mode Phase 2's rubric already caught in this exact case's original verification (the only bridging material found was generic complex-adaptive-systems theory) — now confirmed independently by attacking the claim's own structure rather than searching for missing prior art.

## Tally: 0 of 3 survive → **REFUTED**
