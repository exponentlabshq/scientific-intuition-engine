## 1. The Domain

Auction theory investigates how different auction formats influence bidder behavior and the final sale price of goods. It analyzes the strategies that bidders use, the impact of competition, and the role of information asymmetry in determining outcomes.

## 2. The Proposition

The load-bearing assumption in auction theory is that bidders act rationally to maximize their utility, resulting in predictable bidding strategies and outcomes.

The exact opposite is true: bidders act irrationally, leading to unpredictable bidding strategies and outcomes.
