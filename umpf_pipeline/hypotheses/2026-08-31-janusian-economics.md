## 1. The Domain

Market microstructure examines how securities are traded, focusing on the mechanisms of order books that record buy and sell orders. It analyzes how these interactions shape price discovery, liquidity, and the overall efficiency of financial markets.

## 2. The Proposition

The load-bearing assumption in this field is that the order book reflects market efficiency, as the aggregation of diverse orders provides a clear signal of supply and demand, leading to optimal price formation.

## 3. The Inversion

The exact opposite is true: the order book reflects market inefficiency, as the aggregation of diverse orders creates noise that obscures true supply and demand, leading to suboptimal price formation.
