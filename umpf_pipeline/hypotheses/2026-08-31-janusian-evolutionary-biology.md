## 1. The Domain

Punctuated equilibrium is a theory in evolutionary biology which posits that species evolve through periods of rapid change followed by long intervals of stability. This challenges the traditional view of gradualism, where evolution is seen as a slow and continuous process.

## 2. The Proposition

The load-bearing assumption in evolutionary biology is that evolutionary change occurs gradually over time, with small, incremental changes leading to the development of new species.

## 3. The Inversion

The exact opposite is true: evolutionary change occurs in rapid bursts, with long periods of stability in between.
