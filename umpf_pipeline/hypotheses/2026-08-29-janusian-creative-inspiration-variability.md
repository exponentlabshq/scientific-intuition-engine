## 1. The Domain

Creative inspiration variability refers to the fluctuations in the sources and intensity of creative ideas experienced by individuals or groups over time. This variability can be influenced by numerous factors, including emotional state, environmental context, and social interactions, leading to periods of high creativity followed by lulls.

The load-bearing assumption in this field is that creative inspiration is predominantly influenced by external stimuli and environmental factors.

## 3. The Inversion

The exact opposite is true: creative inspiration is predominantly influenced by internal psychological states and individual cognition.

- **(A) Compromise**: Creative inspiration is influenced by either external stimuli or internal psychological states, but not both at the same time.

(C) is the paradox because it asserts that both propositions are true at once, without resolving the contradiction into a blend. (A) fails as it suggests a sequential influence rather than simultaneous, while (B) resolves the tension by implying a hierarchy of influence.
