## 1. The Domain

Physical ecosystem succession describes the gradual process through which ecosystems evolve following a disturbance, characterized by a series of stages where different species colonize and dominate the area. This process leads to changes in biodiversity and community structure over time.

## 2. The Proposition

The load-bearing assumption in this field is that ecosystem succession is a linear and predictable process, where each stage leads to a more complex and stable community.

## 3. The Inversion

The exact opposite is true: ecosystem succession is a chaotic and unpredictable process, where stages can regress or change in unexpected ways.
