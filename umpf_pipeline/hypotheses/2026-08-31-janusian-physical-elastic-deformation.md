## 1. The Domain

Physical elastic deformation refers to the reversible change in shape or size of materials when subjected to stress, such as stretching or compressing. When the applied stress is removed, the material returns to its original form, demonstrating the principles of elasticity as described by Hooke's Law.

The load-bearing assumption in this field is that elastic deformation is a linear process, meaning the amount of deformation is directly proportional to the applied stress within the elastic limit of the material.
