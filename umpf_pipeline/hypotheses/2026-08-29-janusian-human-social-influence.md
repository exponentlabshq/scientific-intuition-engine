## 1. The Domain

Human social influence encompasses the ways in which individuals affect one another's thoughts, feelings, and behaviors within social contexts. This includes mechanisms like conformity, persuasion, and social norms that shape group dynamics and individual decision-making.

## 2. The Proposition

The load-bearing assumption in this field is that social influence primarily operates through mechanisms of conformity, where individuals adjust their behaviors to align with group norms and expectations.

## 3. The Inversion

The exact opposite is true: social influence primarily operates through mechanisms of individual defiance, where individuals reject group norms and assert their own beliefs and behaviors.

## 4. The Simultaneous Hold

> "Social influence primarily operates through mechanisms of conformity, where individuals adjust their behaviors to align with group norms."
> "Social influence primarily operates through mechanisms of individual defiance, where individuals reject group norms and assert their own beliefs."
> "Both are true simultaneously."

- **(A) Compromise**: Social influence can involve both conformity and defiance, but one may be more prominent depending on the situation.
- **(B) Synthesis**: Social influence is a blend of conformity and defiance, with individuals sometimes conforming and sometimes resisting.
- **(C) Paradox**: Social influence manifests as individuals conforming to group norms while simultaneously defying them in the same social interaction, reflecting a duality of behavior.

(C) is the genuine paradox because it illustrates that in a single social interaction, an individual can both conform to group expectations and simultaneously assert their individuality, thus holding both behaviors concurrently. (A) and (B) fail to meet the criteria for Janusian thinking because they suggest a separation of behaviors rather than a simultaneous coexistence.

## 5. The Hypothesis (The Third Thing)

**If both social influence through conformity and social influence through individual defiance hold simultaneously, then individuals will exhibit behaviors that reflect both alignment with group norms and personal individuality in the same social interaction — which would not be predicted by either truth held alone.**
