Creative block is a phenomenon where individuals experience an inability to produce new ideas or work creatively, often attributed to psychological, emotional, or environmental factors. It is widely recognized in fields such as art, writing, and innovation, where creativity is essential for success.

The load-bearing assumption in this field is that creative block is primarily a negative state that hinders the creative process and must be overcome to achieve creative output.

The exact opposite is true: creative block is primarily a positive state that enhances the creative process and should be embraced as part of creativity.

**If both creative block hinders creativity and enhances it simultaneously, then individuals who experience creative block may produce more innovative work after engaging with the block than those who do not experience it — which would not be predicted by either truth held alone.**
