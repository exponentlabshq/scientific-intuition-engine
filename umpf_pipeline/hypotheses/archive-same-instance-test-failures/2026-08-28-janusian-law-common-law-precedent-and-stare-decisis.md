## 1. The Domain

In common law systems, legal decisions made by higher courts create binding precedents that lower courts are obligated to follow, a principle known as stare decisis. This ensures consistency and predictability in the law, allowing similar cases to be decided in a similar manner.

The load-bearing assumption in this field is that adherence to precedent (stare decisis) is essential for maintaining legal stability and predictability.
