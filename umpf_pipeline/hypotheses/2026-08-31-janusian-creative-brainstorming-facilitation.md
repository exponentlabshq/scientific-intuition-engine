## 1. The Domain

Creative brainstorming facilitation involves guiding groups in generating innovative ideas collaboratively. Facilitators employ various techniques to stimulate creativity and ensure that participants feel comfortable sharing their thoughts, aiming to create an environment conducive to free expression and idea development.
