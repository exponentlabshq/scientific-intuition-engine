## 1. The Domain

Human emotional fluctuation refers to the natural variations in a person's emotional state over time, influenced by external stimuli, internal thoughts, and physiological changes. Researchers study these fluctuations to understand their implications for mental health, behavior, and interpersonal relationships.

The exact opposite is true: emotional states are inherently unstable and fluctuate randomly, independent of identifiable triggers.
