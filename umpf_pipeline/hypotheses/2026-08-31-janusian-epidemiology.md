## 1. The Domain

Epidemiology investigates how diseases spread within populations, examining factors such as transmission routes, host characteristics, and environmental influences to understand outbreak dynamics. This field aims to inform public health strategies and control measures to mitigate the impact of infectious diseases.

## 2. The Proposition

The load-bearing assumption in epidemiology is that disease outbreaks spread primarily through direct contact between infected and susceptible individuals.

## 3. The Inversion

The exact opposite is true: disease outbreaks spread primarily through indirect means, such as environmental reservoirs or vectors, rather than direct contact.

(C) is the paradox because it asserts that both direct and indirect transmission can occur simultaneously and contribute to the same outbreak event, while (A) and (B) fail to hold the contradiction as they suggest a resolution that prioritizes one mode over the other.
